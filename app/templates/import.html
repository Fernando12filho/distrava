Refreshing your data
Apple Health · a few minutes on your iPhone
  1. On your iPhone, open Health and tap your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  2. Tap Export All Health Data, then Export. A long history can take several minutes.
  3. Send export.zip to this Mac — AirDrop is quickest — and drop it above.
  • Re-importing is safe: activities already here are skipped, never duplicated.
  • It also corrects each activity's local time using the time zone recorded by your watch, replacing the estimate made for activities imported earlier.
  • GPX files only carry UTC, so they get this computer's time zone. CSV times are used as written.
