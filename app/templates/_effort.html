Your own 0–10 rating of the whole session (the CR-10 scale). Load is the rating × minutes, the same unit for runs and lifts. Up to 2 counts as easy and 5 or more as hard; those cut-offs come from the scale's own labels, so treat them as a convention. Heart rate under-counts lifting effort, which is why the rating is preferred for gym sessions.
