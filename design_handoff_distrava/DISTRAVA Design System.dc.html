05 — Decisions
DSV
DS-01 · 2026-10-22
Short wordmark: DSV
When the sidebar is collapsed the wordmark becomes DSV, Inter 800 18px. The lime mark moves from the I to the S — still exactly one accent letter.
DS-02 · 2026-10-22
Collapsible sidebar → 72px icon rail
The sidebar can be hidden to a 72px rail: DSV, the toggle, one icon per live page (hover shows its name), and the status dot. Icons are 16px outline, 1.3px stroke, round caps; the active item keeps its lime bar. "Soon" items and the footer text are hidden on the rail. The open sidebar stays text-only, as handed off. The choice is remembered per machine.
Motion: contents fade out (120ms), width glides (220ms, cubic-bezier(0.2, 0, 0, 1)), contents fade in (120ms). Instant when the OS asks for reduced motion.

DS-03 · 2026-10-22
Dashboard opens with Today
The first card on the dashboard is today's plan from the coach week: one tile per session (kind, status chip with the board's wording, focus, every exercise with its volume). A tile opens that session on the Coach board. Nothing planned shows one quiet line and a link — no red, no "missed".

DS-04 · 2026-10-22
Coach page: live chat, closable panel, week that never spills
Asking the coach never reloads the page: the question appears at once with a pulsing "Coach is thinking…", then the board and panel re-render in place (also Accept, Discard, Undo and drag-to-move). The panel closes to a 56px rail with the DS-02 motion. Day columns never go below 100px — the week scrolls sideways instead — and tighten their padding when the board is under 1000px. DS-05 · 2026-10-27
One coach chat across weeks
The Coach panel shows the same conversation on every week's page, so planning weeks ahead never loses the chat. A message asked while viewing another week carries a small mono "WEEK OF …" label above its bubble. A "+" icon button in the panel header starts a new chat (and drops a preview still waiting); earlier per-week messages were kept as one earlier conversation.
DS-06 · 2026-10-27
Week info on the board, the Coach panel is only chat
Today, Hard sets and Week check describe the week, so they sit in one strip between the week heading and the day columns, side by side and wrapping (min 220px each) when the board is narrow; each card keeps its own height. The Coach panel holds only the conversation.

DS-07 · 2026-10-27
Coach chat styled after VS Code's chat
Your messages sit in a bubble on the right; the coach's replies run the panel's full width. The composer is one rounded box (12px radius, accent border on focus) holding a borderless text area and a 30px accent send arrow. The text area has no resize handle: it grows with what you type up to about 8 lines, then scrolls inside. Enter sends and Shift+Enter makes a new line, as in Claude and ChatGPT; a one-line hint under the box says so. While the coach works, the thread's pulsing "Coach is thinking…" is the loading state and the arrow is disabled.
